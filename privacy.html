Privacy

A short, plain explanation of what this site does with data. There is no tracking here, so this is a quick read.

Nothing, by default. There are no analytics scripts, no ad trackers, and no forms on this site that collect personal information. Browsing the site does not send anything about you anywhere.

Your choice of dark or light mode is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it next time. This stays on your device. It is never sent to me or to anyone else.

Videos in the Work section do not load until you click play. Once you do, if the video is hosted on YouTube, YouTube may set its own cookies and collect data according to its own privacy policy, not mine. You can read that at policies.google.com/privacy.

The "Copy username" button on this site only copies text to your clipboard. It doesn't send me anything. I only see a message from you if you actually add me and message me on Discord, and at that point Discord's own privacy policy applies to that conversation.

If anything here is unclear, message me on Discord and I'll answer directly.

Chris, Roblox developer.
