Policies

How a commission works, from first message to delivery.

Send me a message with:

  1. Your game or project
  2. The systems you need built
  3. Your budget and deadline
  4. Any references or examples

The more detail you give me up front, the faster I can scope the work and give you an accurate quote.

For most projects, I ask for half the agreed price upfront to begin work, with the remainder due on delivery. For small fixes or short jobs, I ask for full payment upfront. Larger projects can be split into milestones instead. Payment method and schedule are always agreed on before any work starts.

A reasonable round of revisions is included so the system matches what we agreed on. Changes outside the original scope, like new features that weren't part of the request, are quoted separately.

Once a project is paid in full, full ownership of the scripts and systems transfers to you, along with the source files. I keep the right to show the work in my own portfolio unless you ask me not to, in which case I'm happy to keep it private.

Deadlines are set before work begins, based on the scope of the project. If anything is going to run late, I'll tell you as soon as I know, not after the deadline has passed.

Discord is the main way to reach me. I aim to reply within a day or two, and I'll give you progress updates as the work moves forward rather than going quiet until it's done.

Before work starts, a refund is straightforward. Once work is underway, refunds are handled case by case based on how much progress has been made. If something isn't working as agreed, my first step is always to fix it, not to walk away.
